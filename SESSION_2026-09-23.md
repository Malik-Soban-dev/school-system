### Finance definition record

Commit: `b51819b`

Recorded the proposed finance semantics in `PROGRESS.txt`:

- Billed fees are invoice amounts, not revenue or profit.
- Collected fees are recorded payment receipts.
- Outstanding is billed minus recorded receipts, never below zero.
- Overdue is the unpaid balance after the due date.
- Expenses are recorded expenses by paid date.
- Payroll disbursements are recorded salary payments, not gross payroll liability.
- Scholarships and concessions reduce issued invoice amounts but are not collected cash.
- Refunds, voids and chargebacks are not silently netted into profit because the current ledger lacks complete immutable reversal treatment.

- Branch financial rankings are not implemented.
- Refund, void, chargeback and immutable reversal treatment is incomplete.
- Recorded online/manual receipts are not automatically treated as settled cash.

- The Cypress wrapper has repeatedly hung before issuing browser requests in recent runs. Earlier branch workflows passed 15/15, but the latest financial batches do not claim a new Cypress pass.
