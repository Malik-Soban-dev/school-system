## Next batch definition record - current finance semantics for review
- Billed fees: the recorded invoice amount for the selected school, branch and period. This is not revenue or profit.
- Collected fees: the sum of recorded school payment receipts linked to those invoices. A recorded online/manual receipt is not treated as settled cash without reconciliation evidence.
- Outstanding fees: max(billed fees - recorded collected receipts, 0) for the same scope.
- Overdue fees: the unpaid portion of an invoice whose due date is before the reporting date; partial payments leave only the unpaid portion overdue.
- Expenses: recorded school expense amounts by branch and paid date.
- Payroll disbursements: recorded salary-payment amounts by branch and payment date; payroll liability or gross payroll is not treated as cash paid.
- Scholarships and concessions: reduce the invoice amount when applied to an issued invoice; they are not collected cash and will remain separately identified in later statements.
- Refunds, voids and chargebacks: the current school ledger has no complete immutable reversal treatment, so they must not be silently netted into profit. Profit and branch ranking remain unavailable until reversal and settlement rules are approved.
- Status: proposed current semantics for owner review; no monthly/yearly statement or profit calculation should be implemented until these rules and unsupported reversal cases are approved.
